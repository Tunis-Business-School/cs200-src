Suppose you are working on a recipe website. 
Recipes have a title, a list of ingredients, and an instructions section. 
Make a webpage that shows the recipe for grilled cheese sandwiches with ham and tomato. 
The ingredients should be displayed as an unordered list, with no bullets. 
Meat ingredients should have a light red background color.
Vegetables should have a light green background
Dairy products should have a light yellow. 
The title should be in a sans-serif font of your choice
The instructions should have a heading “Instructions” and the font for the instructions should be italic.
